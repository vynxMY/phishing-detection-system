Security awareness

Short lessons to spot phishing in everyday email.

Check the real URL

Hover before you click. The text you see may not match the actual destination. Watch for extra subdomains, misspellings, and unusual endings like .xyz or .top.

Sender vs Reply-To

Attackers often display a trusted name while routing replies elsewhere. Compare the From domain with Reply-To and Return-Path when headers are available.

Urgency is a tactic

Messages that demand immediate action — “account suspended”, “final notice”, “within 24 hours” — are designed to skip your normal caution.

Authentication is evidence

SPF, DKIM, or DMARC failures are warning signals, not absolute proof. Combine them with URL and sender checks before deciding.
