Explainability

PhishGuard does not stop at a score. After Logistic Regression predicts phishing vs legitimate, the interface shows the signals that influenced that decision.

How a decision is explained

1
You submit a URL or email. The engine extracts TF-IDF text features, URL features, and metadata.
2
Logistic Regression outputs a phishing probability and a risk classification.
3
Analysis signals (URL structure, sender mismatch, urgent language, and related scores) are shown as contribution bars.
4
A short narrative and security advice translate those signals into something a non-expert can act on.

Contribution bars use the analyser signal scores stored with each scan. They are not claimed as SHAP values.

Suspicious URLs

Hover before you click. Displayed text may not match the real destination. Watch extra subdomains, misspellings, IP addresses, and unusual endings such as .xyz or .top.

Sender mismatch

Attackers often show a trusted name while routing replies elsewhere. Compare the From domain with Reply-To and Return-Path when headers are available.

Urgent / threat language

Messages that demand immediate action — “account suspended”, “final notice”, “within 24 hours” — are designed to skip your normal caution.

What to do next

Each result includes do / do-not advice. Use it with the findings list, then open a printable report if you need a record of the analysis.
