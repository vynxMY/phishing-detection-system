About PhishGuard

An explainable phishing detection system that helps people identify suspicious URLs and emails — then understand why and what to do next.

How detection works

01
SubmitPaste an email or URL into the web app.
02
AnalyseExtract TF-IDF, URL features, and metadata.
03
DetectLogistic Regression evaluates phishing risk.
04
UnderstandSee why it was flagged and what you should do.

Model

Single main model: Logistic Regression.

Features
TF-IDF, URL characteristics, and metadata.
